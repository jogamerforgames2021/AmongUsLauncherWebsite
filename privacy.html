Privacy Policy

We don't collect your data. Here's what that actually looks like.

We collect nothing

No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. We have no servers, no database, and no interest in knowing anything about you. Your name, your IP, what mods you installed, how long you played, none of that goes anywhere.

GitHub serves our files

Game files, mod downloads, news and launcher updates all come from GitHub. When you download something, GitHub's servers log your IP the same way any website does. That's GitHub doing their thing, not us. If you want to know what they do with that, check GitHub's own privacy policy.

Discord integration

The chat tab connects to our Discord server through a read-only embed. The feedback button sends your message to a Discord channel via webhook. That's it. We're not reading your DMs or doing anything weird.

Your stuff stays on your PC

Settings, your game path, installed mods, everything the launcher knows about you lives in AppData on your own machine. Nothing syncs to any server. You can wipe it all from the Uninstall tab anytime you want.

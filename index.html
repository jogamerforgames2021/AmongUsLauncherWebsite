// HOW TO USE
Itch Login Fixer
v1.0  ·  5.9.2026  ·  Temporary fix — not guaranteed to always work.

If Among Us tells you that you don't own the game on itch.io, this app fixes it by writing the right file into your Among Us app data folder so the game skips the ownership check.

  1. Open the app — double-click Itch_Login_Fixer.exe and wait for the window.
  2. Click "Login with itch.io" — your browser opens an itch.io page.
  3. Click "Authorize AU-Launcher" — on the itch page, hit that button.
  4. Wait for "Authorization Complete" — the tab goes dark and shows that message. Stay on it until it does.
  5. Check the fixer window — it should show your profile picture, say "Login fixed!" and the button should read "Success!"
  6. Launch Among Us — the game works normally now. You can close or delete the fixer.
The itch file gets written to your Innersloth folder automatically. Once it says "Login fixed!" the fixer has done its job — you don't need it running anymore.

Okay. So you own Among Us. It's right there in your library. And yet the launcher still slaps you with the **"YOU MUST OWN AMONG US"** error every single time you try to launch the game.

That's stupid. And now it's fixable.

## What causes it

It's an itch.io session problem. The launcher's stored login token either expired or desynced from your account, so it can't prove to the game that you actually own it — even though you do.
